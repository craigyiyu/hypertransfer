How the invitation works

  • 1An invitation email containing a sign-in link will be sent to the patron.
  • 2The patron will create their account using a one-time passcode (OTP) and set a personalized password.
  • 3The patron will then be asked to complete the required KYC information.
  • 4Once KYC passes and final approval is granted, the service will be enabled and the patron can initiate deposits.
